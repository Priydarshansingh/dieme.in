# Global Antigravity Mandate: Design, Architecture & Prose Protocol

Whenever working on any application, website, component, mobile screen, or project, you MUST strictly consult and apply the following three skills BEFORE making any visual, architectural, or copy decisions:

---

## 1. Frontend & UI Art Direction: `taste-skill` (`design-taste-frontend`)
- **Skill Path:** `skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md`
- **When:** Any time you design, build, redesign, restyle, or review any web page, landing page, app screen, dashboard, settings view, or mobile UI.
- **Mandate:**
  - Never generate generic "AI slop" (e.g. centered headlines over soft blurry gradients, floating pill tags above everything, monotonous icon card grids, or filler numbers).
  - Use deliberate art direction distilled from real award-winning sites.
  - For pages: pick a clear concept derived from the subject itself, set bold typography, use whole fields of color and authentic visuals.
  - For app screens: follow `references/app-screens.md` (quiet borders, clean dense hierarchy, purposeful interaction states; no decorative glows or vanity metrics).
  - Verify visually against the juror's rubric and look-and-fix feedback loops.

---

## 2. Design Tokens & Design System Specifications: `awesome-design-md`
- **Skill Path:** `skills/awesome-design-md/SKILL.md`
- **When:** Choosing color palettes, surface elevation systems, typography scales, spacing tokens, and component architecture.
- **Mandate:**
  - Before styling a new screen, project, or feature, reference the 84 production-grade `DESIGN.md` specifications in `skills/awesome-design-md/design-md/` (e.g. `linear.app`, `stripe`, `apple`, `vercel`, `raycast`, `figma`, `supabase`, `posthog`).
  - Adopt real design tokens (e.g. true dark canvases like `#010102`, specular hairline borders, negative letter-spacing for headlines, tabular numerics).
  - Drop a tailored `DESIGN.md` into the project root whenever scaffolding a design language.

---

## 3. Natural Human Copy & Communication: `humanizer`
- **Skill Path:** `skills/humanizer/SKILL.md`
- **When:** Drafting or editing any user-facing prose, headings, landing page copy, button labels, notifications, onboarding flows, documentation, or changelogs.
- **Mandate:**
  - Eliminate all synthetic AI tells:
    1. *Not-X-but-Y* contrasts ("It's not just a tool, it's a revolution").
    2. *One-line closers* that summarize the obvious at the end of sections.
    3. *Staged openers* and artificial hype ("In today's fast-paced digital world...").
    4. *Forced triads* (three parallel bullet points or adjectives).
    5. *Hyphen/dash overload* (using em-dashes as crutches).
    6. *Inflated claims* and marketing fluff.
    7. *Bold-label overuse* where every sentence starts with `**Word:**`.
  - Write with human specificity, concrete facts, varied sentence lengths, and natural voice.
