Workflow Architecture

How Dieme captures live broadcast moments and delivers finished clips to your video editors without cloud rendering lag:

Live Chat Trigger

STAGE 02

STAGE 03
AI Thumbnail

STAGE 04
Telegram Delivery
Editor team receives direct MP4, thumbnail, and 1-click YouTube jump link (?t=...) instantly.
